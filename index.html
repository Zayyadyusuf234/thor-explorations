Independent analysis · Thor Explorations · Segilola, Osun State

A gold mine that has already produced 82% of its only published reserve.

Thor Explorations has not published a Segilola reserve or resource since March 2021. Everything since is drill results and a scoping study. The company is debt free, pays no income tax at all, and is sitting on the best gold price in history.

Every input is a company filing: the March 2021 reserve of 517,800 oz contained, the disclosed 93.9% plant recovery, and the five separately disclosed annual production figures. The arithmetic is the only thing added. This is not a forecast of closure - it is a statement about what the published disclosure supports. An updated estimate was expected by the end of 2026.

A reserve is not a plan

Mines routinely produce above reserve by converting resource and extending pits. Thor is drilling hard below the Segilola pit and the intercepts are genuinely good - 19.4 m at 3.01 g/t, 2 m at 9.27 g/t, open at depth. The point is not that conversion cannot happen. It is that on today's disclosure, none of it has.

The royalty nobody has priced

Nigeria charges gold royalty as a fixed naira sum per ounce, not the 3% ad valorem that gets quoted. The rate doubled on 1 July 2025 and collection moved to the Nigeria Revenue Service on 1 January 2026.

Royalty per ounce of gold, from the disclosed royalty expense over disclosed ounces. Two law firms state the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 took gold to a 15% ad valorem rate from January 2026; the company's own H1 2026 charge is 2.3% of revenue, which contradicts that. The model carries both bases rather than choosing.

One more thing most models leave out entirely: export supervision, customs, cadastre and aviation handling fees came to $4.35m in FY2025, about $46 an ounce - larger than the royalty was that year. Gold doré is air-freighted.

What is holding it up

Gold price FY2028 needs to fund itself

$4,552 against the company's own long-term deck of $3,500

The group funds Douta from cash on base case - but only because it banks a year of cash at a gold price near $4,600 first. At its own long-term assumption, that buffer is not there. The mine itself stops making money only below $1,563 an ounce, which is the honest counterpoint: Segilola is a genuinely good asset.

How the model was built

The central moves are to separate ounces that are reserves from ounces that have to convert, to model the royalty on the basis the company actually discloses rather than the one everyone quotes, and to treat the tax holiday as a scenario because its expiry is nowhere in the filings.

Fourteen tabs, 1,402 formulas, 33 automated integrity checks, FY2024 to FY2036, in US dollars because that is how the company reports. The ounce account is the spine: opening reserve, plus conversion, plus exploration, less production, equals closing reserve, with the stockpile drawn on only once the pit cannot fill the plant.

The depletion calculation, and what it does and does not claim

The 2021 probable reserve of 517,800 oz contained, at the disclosed 93.9% recovery, gives 486,214 oz recoverable. Production poured from FY2022 to H1 2026 - five separately disclosed figures - totals 398,991 oz, which is 82.1% of it. This is arithmetic on published numbers, not a company statement, and it is labelled as such on the register. It does not claim the mine is closing. It claims the published reserve no longer supports the production profile, which is a different and much narrower statement.

Why the royalty is modelled two ways

Thor's accounts state a royalty of a fixed naira sum per ounce produced, doubled from ₦16,218 to ₦32,436 on 1 July 2025. Realised cost went from about $14/oz in FY2024 to $31/oz in FY2025 to roughly $90–109/oz in H1 2026. Two advisory firms state that the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 moved gold to 15% ad valorem from January 2026, but Thor's own H1 2026 charge of 2.3% of revenue is inconsistent with that. Rather than pick, the model carries both bases behind a switch and stresses the ad valorem case.

Four inconsistencies inside the company's own FY2025 filing

Profit before tax is stated as $196.211m in the income statement but the tax reconciliation opens at $199.317m. Capital expenditure appears as $4.977m in the plant note and $9.879m as sustaining capital in the results release, on different definitions. The pioneer service charge appears as both $4.358m and $3.075m. And the stated royalty rate per ounce does not reconcile to the reported royalty expense. All four are recorded on the register rather than silently resolved; the model works from realised figures where the stated rates do not tie.

What is not modelled

Senegalese fiscal terms for Douta are not modelled - a mining convention was still under discussion with the government as at August 2026. Working capital is not forecast. The capital allowance position under the new uniform schedule is not modelled, because it is unclear whether the 95% mining initial allowance survived the Nigeria Tax Act. Cash above a ceiling is distributed rather than accumulated, which is a modelling convention rather than company policy, adopted so the forecast does not build a cash pile that would flatter finance income.
